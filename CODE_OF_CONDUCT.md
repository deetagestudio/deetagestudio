# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Deetage Studio Pvt Ltd is committed to maintaining a professional, respectful and welcoming environment for everyone participating in our projects.

We expect all contributors and community members to communicate respectfully and constructively.

## Expected Behaviour

We encourage:

* Respectful and professional communication
* Constructive feedback
* Collaboration and knowledge sharing
* Consideration for different perspectives
* Responsible reporting of issues
* Respect for project maintainers and contributors

## Unacceptable Behaviour

The following behaviour is not acceptable:

* Harassment or discrimination
* Personal attacks or insults
* Threats or intimidation
* Deliberate disruption of discussions
* Publishing private or confidential information
* Malicious or abusive behaviour
* Any conduct that violates applicable laws

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or observe unacceptable behaviour, please contact:

**📧 [deetage.studio@gmail.com](mailto:deetage.studio@gmail.com)**

For security vulnerabilities, please use the process described in [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md) rather than public discussions.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may take appropriate action when this Code of Conduct is violated, including removing inappropriate content, restricting participation or taking other reasonable measures.

We aim to handle reports fairly, respectfully and confidentially where appropriate.
